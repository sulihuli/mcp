## Description

SuliHuli is an AI-first electric vehicle catalog. Its remote MCP server gives Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, Gemini
CLI and other agents structured EV data to answer buyer questions with numbers instead of guesses:

- **Range** by test cycle (WLTP, EPA, CLTC) with a WLTP-equivalent, so cars from different markets compare fairly.
- **Charging**: DC 10-80 % time, battery capacity, charging power.
- **Prices** per market and **rankings** with a public criterion: best everyday, long-range, family and performance EVs.
- **Comparisons** of two to four models side by side, with a "which to pick" verdict.

Figures carry a source link and the date it was read. Figures confirmed by a second independent source or a
regulator are marked **verified**; conflicting sources are shown, not averaged. Photos come with author and licence.

Remote Streamable HTTP, read-only, no sign-in, no API key, 60 requests per minute.

## Example prompts

- What is the real-world range and DC charging time of the Hyundai Ioniq 6? Cite sources.
- Compare the Hyundai Ioniq 5 and Kia EV6 on range, charging and price.
- What are the best long-range electric cars right now?
